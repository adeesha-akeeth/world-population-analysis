# World Population Analysis 2022-2025

## Overview
A data analysis project combining two world population datasets (2022 and 2025) 
to calculate real growth metrics and uncover global population trends.

## Questions Answered
1. Which are the top 10 most populated countries in 2025?
2. Which countries grew fastest between 2022 and 2025?
3. Which continents are growing vs shrinking?
4. Which countries added the most people in absolute numbers?
5. Where does Sri Lanka stand in the world? 

## Key Findings
- India stands as the world's most populated country
- Africa is the fastest growing continent at 7.75% average growth
- Oceania is the only shrinking continent at -0.54%
- India added 46 million people in just 3 years
- Sri Lanka ranks #55 globally with 6.4% growth
